👥 Administrators

Every Mystery Mile submitted this quarter is on the public page as soon as it arrives, shown anonymously alongside the others. Visitors guess each one separately.

Remove takes an entry off the page immediately — use it for a blurry photo or a clue that gives too much away. You can put it back afterwards. Reveal publishes the name and moves the entry to Past Mystery Milers.

Download the data

CSV files, ready to open in Excel. Guesses is the detailed one — a row per guess with the voter, their division, what they guessed, and whether it was right.

Voter names appear in the Guesses export only. They are never shown on the public site, and the Mystery Miler never sees who guessed them.

Write-in names to review

Names people typed that aren't on the department roster. Add anyone genuinely missing to the roster so future quarters can score them; ignore the typos.

Divisions

These are what voters pick from, and how the scoreboard groups results. Seeded with the department's ten clinical divisions, plus Fellows, Residents & Trainees, Research and Administrative Staff so everyone who votes has somewhere to put themselves. Edit freely — nothing else depends on these names.

Open & close the forms

These switches take effect immediately for everyone on the site. They are enforced in the database, so closing a form genuinely stops new entries rather than just hiding the page.

Quarterly checklist

The same four moves every quarter. Full write-up in docs/ADMIN-GUIDE.md.

  1. Start of quarter — set the quarter name and dates above, then turn Story submissions on and Mystery Mile voting off.
  2. Collection closes — turn Story submissions off. Review the Pending tab and Feature the stories you want live. Mystery Miles are already on display; skim them and Remove any that don't work.
  3. Voting opens — turn Mystery Mile voting on and announce it.
  4. Reveal — turn Mystery Mile voting off, then use Reveal on each entry on the Mystery Mile tab to publish the names.

Add an administrator

Adding someone here puts them on the access list. They also need a Supabase Auth login before they can sign in — both steps are required.

Second step — create their login.
  1. Open the Supabase dashboard → Authentication → Users → Invite user.
  2. Enter the exact same email address you used above. Casing does not matter, but a typo means they will be turned away at sign-in.
  3. They set a password from the invite email, then sign in at this page.
