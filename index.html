How It Works

Two ways to walk a mile

Walk a Mile is a department-wide campaign built on a simple idea: we work alongside each other for years without knowing much about each other. Every round, colleagues share something real about themselves — some by name, some anonymously — and the rest of us get to know them a little better.

📖
The Conventional Mile
Shared under your name
  1. Pick a theme that fits the story you want to tell.
  2. Answer three short questions in your own words.
  3. We publish it with your name on the intranet and in the newsletter.
Share as many as you like, whenever submissions are open.
👟
The Mystery Mile
Shared anonymously
  1. Photograph your shoes — no faces, no name badges.
  2. Leave three clues about who you are.
  3. The department guesses, and you are revealed at the end of the round.
One entry per person each round, so everyone gets a turn.
How a round runs
PHASE 1
Submissions open
Share a Conventional Mile, a Mystery Mile, or both. Watch for Lucy's announcement.
PHASE 2
Submissions close
Every Mystery Mile from the round goes up on the board, anonymously, with its clues.
PHASE 3
Guessing opens
Add your name and division once, then guess each shoe. One guess per Mystery Miler, and guesses are final.
PHASE 4
The reveal
Identities are announced, and we recognise the division with the most correct guesses.
Questions, or want to know more?

Get in touch with Dr. Donna-Ann Thomas at donna-ann.thomas@yale.edu. She is happy to talk through the campaign, how your story would be used, or anything you would like to see included.
