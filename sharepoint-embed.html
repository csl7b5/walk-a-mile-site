The Process

How It Works

✍️
1
Submit Your Mile
Choose Conventional (named story) or Mystery Mile (anonymous clues + shoe photo). Takes ~5 minutes.
⭐
2
Editorial Review
The Office of Collaborative Excellence selects 1–2 features per campus each month.
🗞️
3
Published & Shared
Your story appears here on the intranet and in the monthly department newsletter.
👟
4
Mystery Revealed
Mystery Milers stay anonymous all month. The reveal drops on the last day — can your colleagues guess?
